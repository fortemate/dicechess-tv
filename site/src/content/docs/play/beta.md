Dice Chess for Fire TV is in a tester round before its release. The builds are pre-releases: free, in English, and still changing.

## On a Fire TV Stick: Live App Testing

Owners of a Fire TV Stick 4K Select or a Fire TV Stick HD (2nd generation) can install the game from the Amazon Appstore by invitation, without developer mode or a computer:

1. Write to [hello@fortemate.com](mailto:hello@fortemate.com) with the email address of the Amazon account your Stick is registered to. Amazon asks that testers be at least 18, so a child plays on a parent's Stick.
2. Amazon sends an invitation to that address, and a notification to the Stick.
3. Open the link in the email for your country's Appstore, choose your Stick under **Send to Device**, and the game installs like any other app.

If the game does not appear, open **Settings → My Account → Sync Amazon Content** on the Stick. The Stick is listed only if it is sold in your country's Amazon store.

## In developer mode or on the Virtual Device

Each [pre-release on GitHub](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/releases) carries three packages: one for a Fire TV Stick in developer mode, and two for the Vega Virtual Device, on a Mac with Apple silicon and on Linux or an Intel Mac. Installing them needs the `vega` command line from Amazon's Vega SDK. The [playtest guide](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/blob/main/docs/playtest.md) has the steps.

## What to try

- **How to play**, the five-lesson tutorial, before anything else.
- A game against the computer: Rolly (easy), Grabby (medium) or Rampage (hard).
- A hotseat game with someone else in the room, passing the remote.
- **Rules**, to look something up.
- The music as a game gets tense, and **Settings** for the music and the sound effects.

## Tell us what you found

- **Without an account:** the [anonymous form](/dicechess-tv/feedback/).
- **With a GitHub account:** [Discussions](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/discussions). Tell us what happened in a game under **General**, share an idea under **Ideas**, and ask for help with installing under **Q&A**.

Say which build you played, and whether on a Fire TV Stick or on the Virtual Device. What confused you is as useful as what broke.

## What this build is

- **Tested on** the Vega Virtual Device so far, and not yet on a Fire TV Stick.
- **Private:** it has no network code, no accounts and no analytics. Games, results and settings stay on the TV.
- **English only** for now.
