Last updated: 9 October 2026.

This policy covers Dice Chess, the game for Amazon Fire TV made by Fortemate. It does not cover this website, or the services of Amazon that the app is installed and runs with.

## In short

This version of the app does not collect, send or share information about you. It has no accounts and no analytics, and it does not connect to the internet. What it remembers stays on your TV.

## What the app keeps on your TV

So that you can carry on where you stopped, the app keeps these in its own storage on the TV:

- the game in progress, with its dice roll and whose turn it is;
- your results against each computer opponent, by the colour you played;
- the number of Hot Seat games won by White, won by Black and drawn, which is not tied to who sat at the remote;
- your settings: sound effects, music and its volume, voices, the Hot Seat host, whether the board turns in Hot Seat, and whether you have answered the offer of the tutorial.

If a saved record can no longer be read, the app keeps a copy of it on the TV rather than deleting it. None of this leaves the TV, and Fortemate cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not connect to the internet or to other devices. Everything it shows and plays comes with the app.
- It does not ask for your name, email address or any account.
- It does not use a microphone or a camera. The characters' voices are recordings that come with the app.
- It does not read your location, your contacts or identifiers of your TV.
- It contains no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising code.

## Children

The game is made for players of every age, children included. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

## Amazon

You get the app from the Amazon Appstore and play it on a Fire TV. Amazon's services, such as your Amazon account, the Appstore, the Fire TV itself and Alexa, handle information under [Amazon's Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.com/privacy), not under this policy. The app plays its sounds through the Fire TV's own media player, which is part of Amazon's system.

## Changes

If a later version of the app handles information differently, this page will say so before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
