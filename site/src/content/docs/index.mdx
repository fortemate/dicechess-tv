<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Family chess with a roll of the dice" icon="random">
    Three dice choose which pieces may move each turn. Luck gives a newcomer a
    real chance against a stronger player, so a family can play one game
    together.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Made for the remote" icon="rocket">
    The arrows jump between the pieces that can move, then between the chosen
    piece's destinations. Over [200 simulated
    games](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/blob/main/scripts/cursor-presses.ts),
    a turn takes 8.7 presses instead of 22.6 square by square.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Three opponents on the TV" icon="puzzle">
    Rolly plays any legal turn at random, Grabby takes the most valuable piece
    it can, and Rampage goes for your king. Each card shows your record against
    it, and you can play either colour.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Music that follows the game" icon="seti:audio">
    Four themes by pepka-prygni: one for the menus, and three that grow tenser
    as your king comes into danger. Settings turns the music and the sound
    effects on or off.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Offline and private" icon="seti:lock">
    The rules, the computer opponents and the saved games all stay on the TV.
    There is no account, and the app's source contains no network calls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open source" icon="open-book">
    The app is AGPL-3.0-only, and it plays by Fortemate's open-source rules
    engine, so anyone can read how a move is judged.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

[Why Dice Chess on TV](/dicechess-tv/project/why/) tells the story, [What it does](/dicechess-tv/project/features/) tours every feature, [How to play](/dicechess-tv/play/how-to-play/) explains the rules, and [Controls](/dicechess-tv/play/controls/) walks through the remote navigation. Try it with [Play the beta](/dicechess-tv/play/beta/). Explore our [Architecture](/dicechess-tv/technology/architecture/), [Platform findings on Vega](/dicechess-tv/technology/vega/), [Performance](/dicechess-tv/technology/performance/), and [Quality & review](/dicechess-tv/quality/). Developers can follow the [Build & run guide](/dicechess-tv/contribute/build/) and check the [Roadmap](/dicechess-tv/roadmap/).

The TV app was built during [Build, Ship, Shape: Amazon Developer Hackathon 2026](https://amazonappdev2026.devpost.com/), in a repository started on 21 September 2026. It plays by the rules of Fortemate's open-source engine, [`@fortemate/dicechess-engine`](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-engine), which existed before. The [friction log](/dicechess-tv/friction-log/) records the obstacles we met in Amazon's Vega SDK along the way.
