# Status and evidence, October 2026

Until October 20, 2026, the README carried these dated status lines under its badges. The README is a front page now, and the lines live here, split by date and device, with the pull-request numbers that record each one. Unless a line names the Stick, what it describes was seen on the Vega Virtual Device, and the emulator does not measure Stick performance. Evidence from the Virtual Device is not evidence from a Fire TV Stick, and neither is a passing test.

## Builds, launches and what is implemented

- **A React Native for Vega application that builds and runs from this repository.** `mise run build` produces an installable package that installs, launches and plays on the Vega Virtual Device and, since October 6, 2026, on a Fire TV Stick 4K Select (Vega OS 1.2).
- **Fire TV Stick, October 6, 2026:** Amazon's KPI Visualizer measured a cool start of a Release build at a median of 412 ms to first frame and 2.65 s to fully drawn (9 launches, not in certification mode), against Amazon's cool-start targets of under 1.5 s and under 8 s; the [Performance](https://dicechess-tv.fortemate.com/technology/performance/) page has the warm starts too.
- **Virtual Device, September 25, 2026:** the same tool measured 309 ms and 748 ms on average (3 iterations, before the music, voices and portraits; [FL-20](https://dicechess-tv.fortemate.com/friction-log/#fl-20)).
- **Implemented on the native board, driven entirely by D-pad, OK and Back:** Play a friend and three local opponents, from easy to hard, three-die turns, promotion, king capture, resignation, draw agreement, save and resume mid-turn, a completed-game ledger, an interactive tutorial offered on the first launch, and a rules guide.
- **A Fire TV Stick 4K Select with Vega OS** was bought for testing and has been in use since October 6, 2026 ([#10](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/issues/10)); Samsung/Tizen, Raspberry Pi and further hardware purchases are deferred. After the hackathon, the first priority is more TV platforms, starting with Fire OS ([roadmap](roadmap.md), M7).

## Seen on the Vega Virtual Device

- **Sound for every step of the game**, chosen by ear, heard on the virtual device, and set on a settings screen in both menus; an icon and splash screen; and an About screen carrying the credits the asset licenses require.
- **Adaptive music** follows the danger to the king, with four themes by pepka-prygni used with his permission. On the virtual device it was heard following a recorded game against Rampage from calm to tense to critical, and its own reports show it stopping for the launcher ([#76](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/issues/76)).
- **Motion:** each move slides to its new square, so an opponent's turn can be followed ([#131](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/issues/131)), a roll tumbles in ([#99](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/issues/99)), and the board can turn to the side to move in a game against a friend ([#120](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/issues/120)).
- **Highlights** in Settings offers four board-highlight presets: **Standard** (default), **Enhanced** (lifted piece in hand and bold two-tone cursor frame), **Color-blind** (Okabe–Ito palette with shape redundancy and ghost preview), and **High contrast** (monochrome black and white with shape redundancy). They were evaluated on the browser test bench ([#402](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/402)), are covered by unit tests, and were installed and launched on the Fire TV Stick 4K Select in build 32 ([#413](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/413)).
- **Auto-play only move**, off by default, automatically plays the only legal move. The first rule, which also picked up a lone piece with several squares, was checked on the virtual device and on the Stick ([#342](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/342)), and its narrowing to the only move on the virtual device ([#361](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/361)).
- **The host's line:** while the host speaks in a game against a friend, she shows with her line above the bottom badge ([#213](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/issues/213): seen on the Vega Virtual Device and checked by tests, not yet checked on a Fire TV Stick).
- **The turn controller:** it follows each roll through the engine's legal turn tree, so a turn is checked as a whole, and takes the dice left from the engine's `applyMove` (engine 0.13.0, [#101](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/101)). `test/game.test.ts` and `test/dice.test.ts` cover both, and hotseat, bot and promotion turns were played this way on the Vega Virtual Device, and turns against Grabby on a Fire TV Stick ([#326](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/326)). Which of the dice left a legal turn can still spend, and so which dice dim, is the engine's `getPlayableDice` (engine 0.14.0, [#140](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/140)), covered by `test/dice.test.ts` and checked on the Vega Virtual Device; not yet checked on a Fire TV Stick.
- **The bots:** Rolly, Grabby and Rampage compute their reply on the JavaScript thread and have not needed a thread of their own on the Vega Virtual Device.

## Seen on the Fire TV Stick 4K Select

- **Dice stagger:** a roll's second and third dice came in late until their stagger moved onto the native driver; the owner played that build there and reported that it works ([#306](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/306)).
- **Board memoization:** memoizing the board cut the median time from a cursor key to the frame that shows it from 152 ms to 59 ms there ([#326](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/326)).
- **October 9, 2026:** the app's reports showed it stopping at Home and taking `focus` as its return, which is how the Stick signalled it then ([#296](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/296)).
- **October 10, 2026:** the Appstore package of build 26 was checked on the Stick: the Settings rows, muting and unmuting the music on its row, Play as on Random, Main menu and Resume game, the hint on the opponent cards, and the confirmation before a game in play is replaced, with Cancel ([release notes](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/releases/tag/v1.0.0%2B26)).
- **October 11, 2026:** the Appstore package of build 28 was installed there over an earlier build without uninstalling: it kept the saved game, showed the launch splash with the redrawn hand and then the home menu, and the device log showed no errors from the app ([release notes](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/releases/tag/v1.0.0%2B28)).
- **October 14, 2026:** the [audio validation record](audio-regression-validation-387.md) describes the playback lifecycle fixes tested in [PR #388](https://github.com/fortemate/dicechess-tv/pull/388), with separate VVD and physical Stick results, packaging checks and the limits of the evidence.
- **Menu button:** opened and closed the game menu on a Fire TV Stick, as [the cycling validation](board-navigation-validation-299.md) records; covered by tests; not yet checked on the Vega Virtual Device, where F2 is its expected key.
